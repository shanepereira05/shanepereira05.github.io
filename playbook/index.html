What’s inside

Seven modules, built around the actual week of a PM.

Every module is written to be applied the same day you read it. No frameworks for the sake of frameworks. Each one includes the exact prompts, the gotchas, and the before/after of a real example from my own work.

  2. Discovery, compressed

    Reading 200 Jira tickets in five minutes. LLM-assisted competitive research without hallucinated metrics. The MCP setup that reads across your Jira, Confluence and Slack in one prompt.

  3. Writing specs with AI

    14 pages · 30 minutes

    The PRD scaffolding prompt I use every week. The “sharpen my thinking” prompt that catches gaps before engineering review. Four failure modes to watch for, with the fix for each.

  4. Measurement & telemetry

    9 pages · 20 minutes

    Interpreting dashboards with an LLM as a sanity check, not a crutch. The “why is this metric moving” workflow. The failure mode where AI confidently invents a cause.

  5. Decision support

    8 pages · 18 minutes

    Scenario modelling as prompts. Prioritisation framework for a team where some work is AI-accelerated and some isn’t. How to keep opportunity cost honest.

  6. The prompt library

    25+ prompts · copy-paste ready

    Every prompt I actually use in a given week, grouped by what it’s for. Battle-tested, not generated. Comes as a Notion template you can fork and edit.

  7. Your first week of AI-native PM

    5 pages · action plan

    A five-day plan you can start Monday. What to try, in what order, with what to measure. Written so you can hand it to a PM on your team and have them actually do it.
